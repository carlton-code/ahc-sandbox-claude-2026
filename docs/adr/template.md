# ADR-NNNN: Short title of the decision

## Status

Accepted

## Context

What situation prompted this decision? What were the realistic alternatives, and what
constraints (technical, team, timeline) shaped the choice?

## Decision

What was actually decided, stated as a single clear sentence, followed by whatever detail is
needed to act on it consistently.

## Consequences

What trade-offs does this decision accept? Include the downsides, not just the upsides — an ADR
that only lists benefits isn't useful when someone later hits the cost side of the trade.
